# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Product managers, software engineers, startup founders, and technical team leads at small-to-mid-size product companies. They work in fast-paced, async environments across time zones. They need to coordinate work across sprints, tasks, and documentation without bouncing between three different tools.

## Product Purpose

Niyoplan is a unified project management SaaS that combines kanban boards, sprint planning, Gantt timelines, documentation, OKRs, and AI tooling in one workspace. It exists to eliminate the coordination overhead that comes from using fragmented tools. Success = a team ships predictably and communicates without friction.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, precise, calm under pressure. Three words: **Focused. Reliable. Purposeful.**

The interface should feel like the tool knows what it's doing — not flashy for its own sake, but visually clear and well-structured. It should project quiet confidence.

## Anti-references

- Linear-clone aesthetics (purple-dominant, heavy dark glassmorphism, particle overload)
- Jira's dense, functional-but-lifeless visual grammar
- Notion's editorial off-white minimal look
- Over-animated dashboards that prioritize motion over content clarity

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't decorate** — every visual element earns its place by communicating something
2. **Calm confidence** — restraint over spectacle; the product's quality speaks louder than effects
3. **Theme integrity** — light mode is first-class, not an afterthought; dark mode is rich, not just inverted
4. **Precision over excess** — tight spacing, clean hierarchy, nothing superfluous
5. **Functional beauty** — UI widgets in the landing page demonstrate real capability, not vaporware mockups

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text must hit ≥4.5:1 contrast against background. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Reduced-motion alternatives for all animations.
